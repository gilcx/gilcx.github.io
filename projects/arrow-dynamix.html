01 / The short version

A bow that does more than shoot.

The idea

The player wakes in a dungeon with a bow and arrow. Shooting objects changes the room, turning aim, movement, and experimentation into the way to discover a path forward.

My contribution

I developed custom C# scripts for movement and physics behavior, helped build arrow-driven object interactions, and coordinated the poster, showcase presentation, and itch.io page.

The release

Our team released 11 levels for browser and Windows play and presented the game at the Queen's Creative Computing Showcase. The itch.io page attracted more than 600 visits.

02 / Gameplay systems

Movement meets experimentation.

Responsive platforming

Player movement and physics scripts support jumping between platforms, avoiding hazards, and positioning for the next shot.

Arrows as interactions

Targets, magnets, barriers, and other objects respond to arrows, giving players a way to manipulate the environment and solve puzzles.

Mechanics in combination

Rooms combine movement, timing, and precise aim. New interactions build on earlier ones as players progress through the dungeon.
